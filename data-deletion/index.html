Data Deletion & Requests

How to contact MasoomDev about app data and support correspondence.

Replay Calculator

Saved formulas and recent calculations are described as stored on your device. We cannot remotely delete data held only on your device. You may use Android app storage controls to clear local data, but this can erase your saved recipes and history.

Support emails

You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Third-party services

Any app-specific third-party data processing must be verified before we can provide more detailed instructions.
