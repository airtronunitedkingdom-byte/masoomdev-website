# MasoomDev AdSense readiness checklist — 6 November 2026

## Completed on this development branch
- Added /contact/ support and privacy enquiries page.
- Linked contact from home, about and privacy.
- Updated website privacy language about local storage and potential future Google advertising. Advertising is NOT active.
- Added /contact/ to sitemap.xml.

## Must verify before submitting
- Decide canonical site: sitemap and robots currently reference https://tools.masoomdev.online; confirm this domain is live, owned, and will be submitted to AdSense. Check canonical tags and redirect behavior on the Vercel URL.
- Review all public game, tool, app and extension pages for unique descriptions, instructions and substantive original value; remove empty/unfinished pages from indexable navigation.
- Test links, navigation, responsiveness, accessibility and browser console on desktop/mobile.
- Review privacy disclosure against actual scripts, storage and analytics; implement legally required consent before personalised advertising where applicable.
- Verify Search Console ownership and that Googlebot can access sitemap, pages, and resources. Check robots and noindex headers.
- Check third-party assets and content licences; no copied content or misleading downloads.
- Create or use an eligible AdSense account; obtain the actual publisher ID from Google. Never insert fabricated pub IDs.
- Add verification script/meta exactly as provided by AdSense, then request site review.
- Only add ads.txt using the real AdSense publisher ID and Google's required line after the account supplies it.
- Keep ads out of gameplay areas, draggable controls, tool inputs, and near download/navigation buttons. Avoid accidental-click placements.
- Monitor policy compliance and ad performance after approval.
