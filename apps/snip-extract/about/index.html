ABOUT THE APP

About Snip & Extract

Snip & Extract is a Windows screenshot capture and OCR text extractor developed by MasoomDev.

How it works

Select a portion of your screen with your mouse. Preview the capture, copy it, save it as a PNG image, or extract text with the Windows OCR engine. The app provides a compact interface and shortcut reference.

Requirements

Windows x64. Text recognition depends on the Windows OCR facilities and language components available on your system.
