MH Player

A lightweight, offline video and audio player for your own device files. No account required.

Features

  • Browse audio and video folders
  • Playback resume, seeking and speed controls
  • Optional equalizer for audio and video
  • Brightness and volume gestures
  • Subtitles, audio tracks and optional background playback
  • Local preferences and playback history

Media compatibility and audio effects depend on Android device capabilities.
