MH Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Developer: MasoomDev · App: MH Player (com.masoomdev.mhplayer)
Privacy at a glance

MH Player plays audio and video files on your device and can play an HTTPS media URL you choose to open. It does not require a MasoomDev account. Media library information, playback positions and player preferences are kept on your device. The app is not designed to upload your local media library to MasoomDev.

1. Information MH Player accesses

Local audio and video: With your permission, MH Player reads information about audio and video files on your device, such as file names, durations, sizes, folder locations and content URIs. This enables media browsing, playlists and playback. You can also choose individual files through the Android file picker.

Playback and preferences: The app stores playback positions, recent items, playlist selections, interface themes, audio settings and other player preferences locally using Android app storage.

Streaming URLs: If you enter an HTTPS media URL, MH Player connects to the remote server to request and play that media. The remote host can receive normal connection information such as your IP address and the requested URL. Its own privacy practices apply. Do not enter private URLs unless you trust the host.

2. Permissions and why they are used

  • Audio and video access: To find and play media stored on your device, subject to Android's permission controls.
  • Internet: To stream media URLs you request and support app-related online functions.
  • Notifications: If allowed, to display media playback controls and app update notifications.
  • Foreground media playback: To support media playback and playback controls when the app is not in the foreground.
  • Audio settings: To support playback volume and audio effects such as equalizer features.

3. Data collection, sharing and third parties

MH Player does not require account registration or cloud synchronization. The current app is not designed to send your local media files, playback history or saved preferences to a MasoomDev server. The app does not include developer-operated advertising or analytics services.

When you request remote streaming, your device communicates with the media host you selected. When the app checks for updates or opens its store page, Google Play or the relevant platform may process information according to its own privacy policy. If you use Android's Share function, the destination app receives only the media you choose to share.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

Playback history and preferences are stored in MH Player's local app storage. You can remove this information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing MH Player's app data does not itself delete your original media files stored elsewhere on your device. Remote media hosts may retain their own connection records under their policies.

5. Security

MH Player supports HTTPS links for user-entered network media. No method of data transmission or storage is completely secure. Only play media from sources you trust, and keep Android and MH Player updated.

6. Children's privacy

MH Player is a general-purpose media utility and is not specifically directed to children. It does not operate a child-focused account or social networking service. Users are responsible for the media sources they open.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when app features or privacy practices change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or MH Player's data practices, contact airtron68@gmail.com.
