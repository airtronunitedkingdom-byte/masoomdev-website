MH Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Developer: MasoomDev

Overview

MH Player is designed to play media stored locally on your Android device. It does not require an account or sign-in.

Files and permissions

With your permission, the app accesses local audio and video files to display your media library and play files you select. The app may also open files you explicitly choose through Android's document picker. Android audio settings access supports audio effects such as the equalizer.

Local information

Playback positions and player preferences (including equalizer settings) are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. They are not uploaded to a MasoomDev server.

Data collection and sharing

The current MH Player application does not include advertising, analytics, account registration or cloud synchronization features. It does not intentionally transmit your media files or personal data to MasoomDev or third parties. No media content is hosted or supplied by the developer.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences and playback positions remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall MH Player. Your original audio and video files are not deleted when you clear app data.

Children

MH Player is a general-purpose media utility and is not specifically directed to children. It does not provide online content.

Changes

If features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app release.
