MH Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Developer: MasoomDev

Overview

MH Player is an Android audio and video player developed by MasoomDev. It plays media stored on your device and can play an online audio or video stream when you provide a URL. No account or sign-in is required.

Information and permissions

Local media: With your permission, MH Player reads audio and video files to display your media library and play media you select. On older Android versions, storage access may be requested for the same purpose. The app can also open media you explicitly select through Android's file picker.

Notifications and playback: Notification permission, where applicable, is used for playback controls and optional app-update alerts. Foreground service permissions support playback while the app is in the background. Audio settings access supports playback-related audio effects.

Internet access: If you choose to play an online stream, MH Player connects to the HTTPS address you enter to retrieve the media. The streaming provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and requests, according to that provider's privacy practices. The app may also check for available updates through Google Play.

Information stored on your device

MH Player stores player settings, playback positions, audio preferences and related app preferences locally on your device. This information is used to remember your playback experience. The app does not require a MasoomDev account or cloud backup.

Data collection, advertising and sharing

The current app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or account registration. MH Player does not upload your local media library or media files to MasoomDev servers. Media is shared externally only when you initiate an Android sharing action or request playback from an external streaming provider. Your use of third-party streams is subject to the relevant provider's practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app preferences and playback positions remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall MH Player. Clearing app data does not delete your original media files. For information processed by a third-party streaming service, consult that service's policy.

Children's privacy

MH Player is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed for children. It does not supply its own online media catalogue.

Security

MH Player uses HTTPS for user-provided online stream URLs. As with any internet connection, the security and privacy of a third-party streaming service depend on that provider.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when MH Player features or data practices change. The updated version and date will be posted on this page.
