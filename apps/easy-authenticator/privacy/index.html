Easy Authenticator Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Easy Authenticator for Android and Windows handles information. It should be read with the app's current version and the account-deletion information linked below.

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

At a glance

  • Authenticator entries and TOTP secrets are stored locally using the operating system's secure-storage facilities.
  • Cloud account and encrypted-vault features are optional. If used, account authentication is handled by Supabase and an encrypted vault envelope may be stored in the cloud.
  • The vault is encrypted on your device before upload. The cloud vault stores the encrypted envelope, not plaintext TOTP secrets.
  • Camera QR scanning and authenticator-code generation are performed on-device.
  • Local authenticator functions can be used offline. Cloud sign-in and sync require an internet connection and cloud configuration.

Information stored on your device

Authenticator entries can include an account label, issuer, TOTP secret, digit count, time period and algorithm. Entries are kept in app storage protected by the device's secure-storage system. The app also stores local preferences such as theme, text size, PIN/biometric settings and backup-scheduling metadata.

If you enable an app PIN, the app stores a salted PIN verifier rather than the PIN in plaintext. Biometric checks are handled by your device's operating system; the app does not receive your biometric template.

Optional cloud account and encrypted sync

If you register or sign in, your email address and account credentials are handled by Supabase Authentication. The app uses the authenticated account identifier to associate your cloud vault with your account.

If you enable encrypted cloud sync or backup, the app encrypts the vault on your device using a password-derived key and AES-256-GCM before sending it to the Supabase backend. The cloud record contains an encrypted envelope and operational fields such as the account identifier and revision number. The encryption format uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with 310,000 iterations to derive the encryption key. Your vault master password and plaintext TOTP secrets are not intentionally sent as cloud-vault contents.

Supabase processes authentication and stores the encrypted vault record on our behalf. Supabase may process technical information such as IP address and request metadata to provide and secure its service; its own privacy and retention terms also apply.

Automatic cloud-backup scheduling and device-held sync credentials are stored on your device. Scheduled backup runs while the app is active/foregrounded; this feature does not rely on a server-side schedule.

Backups and exports

When you export a backup, the app creates a password-protected encrypted file using AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256. You choose where to save or share that file. MasoomDev does not automatically control or delete copies saved outside the app. Keep your backup password safe; anyone with the file and password may be able to restore its contents.

Camera and QR scanning

On supported Android devices, camera access is used to scan authenticator QR codes. QR data is processed by the app to create a local authenticator entry. Transfer QR data can contain authenticator secrets and should not be sent to support.

Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The reviewed app source does not include a dedicated third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Easy Authenticator is not designed to sell personal information. This statement will be updated if future versions add services or integrations.

Sharing and service providers

Supabase processes information only when you use the optional cloud account or encrypted-sync features. Device secure-storage, camera and biometric functions use platform services provided by Android or Windows and the device manufacturer. We do not intentionally share plaintext authenticator secrets with advertising networks.

Retention and deletion

Local authenticator entries and exported backup files remain on your device or wherever you saved them until you remove them. Deleting your cloud account removes the Supabase Auth account and its associated encrypted cloud-vault record. Local entries and exported files are not deleted by cloud-account deletion. Service-provider backups or records may remain for limited periods under applicable provider policies and legal obligations.

You can delete a signed-in cloud account from the app using its cloud-account deletion control and explicit confirmation. If you cannot use the app, follow the web account-deletion instructions. Do not email passwords, PINs, TOTP secrets, recovery codes or backup keys.

Children

Easy Authenticator is a general-purpose security utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality, service providers or data practices change. The revision date above indicates the latest update.
