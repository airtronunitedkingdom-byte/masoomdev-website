How to Create a Clear Website Favicon

Make a small icon recognizable across tabs and devices.

What you need to know

Start with a simple shape or symbol that remains identifiable when reduced to tiny dimensions.

Avoid long words and thin decorative details: they tend to disappear at favicon sizes.

Prepare a square source image, then export appropriate PNG and ICO variants or use an SVG where supported.

Check contrast on both light and dark browser themes, and test the icon at small sizes.

Reference the favicon in your site's HTML and clear caches when checking a newly updated version.

Quick checklist

  • Identify your goal and the required output.
  • Keep an original copy before making changes.
  • Verify the result on the device or service where it will be used.
