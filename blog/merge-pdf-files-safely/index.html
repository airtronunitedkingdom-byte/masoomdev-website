How to Merge PDF Files Safely

Combine documents while checking page order and privacy.

What you need to know

Arrange documents in the required order before merging, especially when combining invoices, forms or application attachments.

Check orientation and page sizes: mixing portrait and landscape pages can be acceptable but may look inconsistent.

For sensitive documents, consider an offline merger rather than uploading files to an unfamiliar service.

After merging, open the result and check every page, bookmarks, links and selectable text.

Keep the original PDFs until you confirm the combined file opens correctly and contains all pages.

Quick checklist

  • Identify your goal and the required output.
  • Keep an original copy before making changes.
  • Verify the result on the device or service where it will be used.
