How to Resize Images for Websites and Online Marketplaces

Resize images while keeping proportions and product details.

What you need to know

Determine the target display dimensions and any marketplace minimum requirements before editing.

Keep aspect ratio locked to prevent stretching. Crop separately when a square thumbnail is required.

For product listings, a clean image with sufficient detail is usually more useful than an excessively compressed thumbnail.

Export at a resolution that supports zooming where required, then inspect the result on desktop and mobile.

Name files descriptively and avoid adding misleading text or decorations to primary product images.

Quick checklist

  • Identify your goal and the required output.
  • Keep an original copy before making changes.
  • Verify the result on the device or service where it will be used.
