Compress Images Without Losing Quality

Use the right format and dimensions before adjusting compression.

What you need to know

Start by checking the image's purpose: a product photograph needs natural detail, while a logo needs crisp edges.

Resize oversized photos to their actual display dimensions. A 4000-pixel image rarely needs to be delivered at full size inside a 900-pixel content column.

For photographs, export JPEG or WebP and compare at the size visitors will see. Increase compression gradually until artifacts appear, then step back.

For logos, text, and transparency, compare PNG against lossless WebP. Always retain an untouched original for later editing.

Inspect fine details such as hair, text, gradients and sharp borders before publishing. Measure file size as well as appearance.

Quick checklist

  • Identify your goal and the required output.
  • Keep an original copy before making changes.
  • Verify the result on the device or service where it will be used.
