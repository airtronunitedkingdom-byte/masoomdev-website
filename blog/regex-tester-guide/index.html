Test Regular Expressions with Examples

Learn how to use the MasoomDev regex tester tool, with practical steps, checks and common mistakes to avoid.

What this tool is useful for

Begin with a small sample that should match. The best results come from understanding your starting material and the format you want to produce. Work on a copy whenever possible, particularly when the input is difficult to replace.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Prepare your input. Begin with a small sample that should match.
  2. Apply the change. Add counterexamples to detect overly broad patterns.
  3. Verify your output. Check flags and escaping rules before using a pattern in production.

How to check your result

Compare the output against your original goal, not just whether the tool reports success. Check the content, format, size and appearance as appropriate. If something looks wrong, return to your original and try smaller adjustments. Different browsers and devices can produce different results, so test in the environment where you plan to use the file or text.

Common mistakes to avoid

Check flags and escaping rules before using a pattern in production. Also avoid overwriting the only copy of your source. For anything confidential, review the tool's privacy implications and prefer an offline workflow when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this on mobile?

Open the tool in a modern mobile browser and check whether its controls fit your screen. Large files and complex tasks may be easier to manage on a desktop.

Should I keep the original?

Yes. Keeping the original makes it possible to correct mistakes and generate another output without accumulating quality loss or losing information.
