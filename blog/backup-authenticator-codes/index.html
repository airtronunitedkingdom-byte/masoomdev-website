How to Back Up Authenticator Accounts Safely

Prepare for phone loss without exposing secret keys.

What you need to know

Before changing phones, check whether your authenticator supports encrypted export or secure account synchronization.

Store recovery codes for each important service independently of the authenticator app.

If exporting a QR code or backup file, treat it like a password: anyone with the secret may generate valid codes.

Test that the new device can generate accepted codes before wiping or selling the old phone.

Keep at least one trusted recovery method available, and periodically review whether it still works.

Quick checklist

  • Identify your goal and the required output.
  • Keep an original copy before making changes.
  • Verify the result on the device or service where it will be used.
