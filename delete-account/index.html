Delete your Easy Authenticator account

Easy Authenticator users can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data using the process below. This page is the web-based route for submitting an account deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Easy Authenticator account, or explain if you can no longer access that address.
  2. Use the subject line Easy Authenticator — Account Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, state that you want your Easy Authenticator account deleted. Do not include your password, authenticator PIN, recovery codes, TOTP secret, or backup key.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request. We will reply to confirm the outcome or explain if we need more information.
Email an account deletion request

Support email: airtron68@gmail.com

What will be deleted

After your request is verified and completed, we intend to delete the Easy Authenticator cloud account and the associated encrypted cloud-vault records linked to that account. Information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons may be retained only as required and handled under the applicable privacy policy.

What is not deleted automatically

Deleting your cloud account does not erase copies you have already exported or data stored locally on your device. Before uninstalling the app, remove any local app data and separately delete exported backup files from your device, computer, or cloud storage if you no longer want to keep them.

Verification and completion

We verify requests to prevent someone else from deleting your account. We will communicate with you by email if verification or clarification is needed and confirm when the request has been processed. Do not send authentication secrets or recovery material as proof of ownership.

Need help?

For questions about this process, contact airtron68@gmail.com. You can also review our Privacy Policy.

This page submits a request by email; it does not delete an account immediately when you open the page or click the email link. Account deletion is performed only after the request is received, ownership is verified, and the deletion process is completed.
