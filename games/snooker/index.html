    Rules & controls

    Pot a red (1 point), then a colour (2–7 points), alternating while reds remain. Colours are respotted until all reds are gone. After the final red and its colour, pot colours in order: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black. A foul awards at least 4 points to the opponent and ends your visit. A legal pot continues your visit; a miss changes turns. Select PvP to play locally with a friend, or PvC to play against the computer. The computer selects available shots automatically. This is a casual snooker implementation: no nominated colours, free-ball, snookers-required, or referee-grade foul-and-miss decisions.

    Drag on the table from the white ball to choose a shot direction. Use the power slider and Shoot. After potting the cue ball, use Place Cue Ball to reposition it in the D. Desktop mouse and mobile touch are supported.
